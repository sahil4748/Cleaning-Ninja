# Cleaning Ninja
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Product Purpose
An Australian cleaning service website that helps visitors understand services, select a package and prepare a cleaning enquiry.

## Users
Homeowners, tenants and commercial clients seeking surface cleaning. Audience inference from the owner's requested Blue Sky service parity.

## Capabilities and Constraints
The owner authorises a completely fresh brand and frontend, all creative and technical decisions, new media, and adopting Blue Sky's service categories and published pricing/deals. Retain existing backend and release safeguards. Existing site contact is contact@cleaningninja.co. There is no verified phone number. No competitor identity, reviews or credentials become Cleaning Ninja facts. Prices cannot be inferred when the reference publishes none. Enquiry uses a user-reviewed email; never imply delivery before sending. Deployment is outside this implementation.

## Brand Commitments
Cleaning Ninja; light olive and beige; premium, cinematic, immersive, tactile, exceptional mobile execution. Layered visual storytelling rather than arbitrary 3D objects. All prior visual choices are superseded. The user explicitly delegates decisions and asks not to wait for design approval.

## Evidence on Hand
Live blueskycarpetcleaning.com.au research for service completeness and offers. Repository operating contact and enquiry boundary. Generated imagery is illustrative brand photography, not evidence of customer work.

## Product Principles
Clarity at every action. Craft at every scale. Service selection should carry into the enquiry. Respect reduced motion and native scrolling.
