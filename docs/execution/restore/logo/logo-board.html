Why this works

The most expensive houses let the name do the work: generous tracking, a single carved serif, one colour, lots of space (Aman’s identity is its logotype; Rosewood pairs a serif wordmark with a quiet mark). Here the mark is a hairline seal rather than an icon, so it stays out of the way, scales from a header to a favicon (a heavier weight is used below 32px), and never looks like a cleaning company. Font: Marcellus (SIL OFL), converted to outlines.
