# Roadmap — recommendations only

1. Foundation: inventory source/routes/claims, record checks and constraints, deliver report; stop.
2. Evidence and readiness: owner confirms services/areas/phone/prices/reviews/credentials; authorize separate lead-delivery, domain/SEO and tooling remediation. Preserve content cleanup and URLs.
3. Homepage approval package: independently composed mobile/desktop layout and cinematic storyboard for Immaculate Transition; cost and fallback plan. No Higgsfield before approval.
4. Approved homepage implementation, then service/location/shared-system rollout; each stage tested against preserved route/content contracts.
5. Integration QA, accessibility/performance/content verification and explicitly approved release.

No dates or estimates are promised. Business evidence and operational lead handling are dependencies, not visual polish tasks.
