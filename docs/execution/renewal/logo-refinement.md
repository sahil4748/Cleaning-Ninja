# Cleaning Ninja — signature refinement

The identity now uses a folded **N** monogram and a custom outlined wordmark. Two rounded outer corners soften the symbol; its open diagonal spaces and cut vertical ends suggest a precise, continuous movement. The silhouette remains legible as a compact single-color mark without a face, mask or sparkle motif.

The lowercase **ninja** lettering is drawn as vector paths, with broad shoulders, generous counters and a single-storey **a**. Compact, spaced **CLEANING** lettering establishes the service above it. The two weights give the name confidence and clarity while fitting the site's restrained interiors direction. All lettering is self-contained vector artwork rather than live font text.

- Header signature: 204px on desktop; 132–154px on smaller screens (about 148px at 390px wide).
- Footer signature: 204px, retaining a stronger signing-off presence.
- Color follows `currentColor`: forest ink on linen, linen on forest. All negative space is transparent, so no background-matching fill is needed.
- The same monogram path is shared by `Mark`, `Brand`, and the standalone SVG favicon asset.
- The signature has one accessible name, “Cleaning Ninja”; the artwork itself is decorative to assistive technology. Existing enclosing home-link labels remain intact.
- `Arrow`, `Mark`, and `Brand` retain their existing exports and call signatures.

This refinement changes only the brand primitives, their scoped stylesheet and the compact SVG asset. Photography, navigation behavior and page content are outside this change.
