# Renewal research

29 September 2026. Live primary-source research informed the new frontend.

## Business content
Blue Sky Carpet Cleaning: https://blueskycarpetcleaning.com.au/ — all eleven categories are represented. https://blueskycarpetcleaning.com.au/specials/ — five packages, up to 30% off. https://blueskycarpetcleaning.com.au/terms-and-conditions/ — room sizes, minimum-charge exclusion and non-combination conditions. No public dollar prices were found. The owner explicitly asked to adopt these services and offers for the new site. Copy was rewritten. Competitor reviews, years in business, identity, phone, guarantees and credentials were not copied.

## Technical references
GSAP ScrollTrigger/matchMedia via current Context7 documentation: https://gsap.com/docs/v3/GSAP/gsap.matchMedia()/ and https://gsap.com/docs/v3/Plugins/ScrollTrigger/. Responsive cleanup uses gsap contexts; reduced-motion and narrow screens get a direct static composition. Native scrolling was selected over Lenis after evaluating https://github.com/darkroomengineering/lenis. No extra motion dependency was installed. Next.js16 getImageProps/picture art direction follows https://nextjs.org/docs/app/api-reference/components/image. Reference images are local optimised assets, with native picture choosing the mobile hero.
