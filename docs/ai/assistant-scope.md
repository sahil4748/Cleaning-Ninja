# Future assistant scope

No assistant integration exists in this branch; none is authorized in foundation. A future assistant may explain verified service inclusions, ask for minimum quote context and direct users to the approved enquiry path.

It must not invent prices, availability, credentials, promises, staff assignments, completed bookings or review evidence. It may claim submission only after a verified backend acceptance. Human escalation should use the owner-approved contact path; phone remains unverified.

Unknowns: business owner for knowledge approval, operating coverage/hours, escalation SLA, consent, retention, provider and integration design. Resolve these before implementation.
