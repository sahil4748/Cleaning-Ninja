# Knowledge policy

Source hierarchy: explicit owner decisions and documented business evidence → verified facts register → approved current content → repository observations. Existing code is evidence of implementation, not proof of a business claim. Historic plans and sample data cannot become AI knowledge by default.

Each publishable claim needs source, owner, verification date, scope and review trigger. Preserve VERIFIED / OWNER-APPROVED / PLACEHOLDER / PENDING / PROHIBITED classifications. On conflict, abstain from the disputed claim and request business evidence; do not infer from a polished page or JSON-LD.

No secrets, customer submissions or access codes in knowledge files. Unknown answers should explain uncertainty and offer the verified contact path. Business changes must update metadata, schema, UI and future AI knowledge together.

Current authority: ../product/owner-decisions.md. Only the public allowlist in content/business-truth.ts may seed future business-truth APIs. No truth endpoint currently exists; never ingest prototype review/team/price/article data as evidence.
