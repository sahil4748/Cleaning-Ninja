Preserve approved simplification from `7124dda396a39e87d7483ba9a03a093c5dce5251`. Never compare with live/main as a reason to restore old content. Current technical readiness authorises narrow identity/proof safeguards; no redesign. See owner-decisions.md.

Use plain Australian English, specific descriptions and a clear next action. Separate a requested time from confirmed availability, an estimate from a binding quote, and a received lead from a confirmed booking. Publish success only after durable acceptance/delivery is verified.

VERIFIED: established evidence. OWNER-APPROVED: current owner direction. PLACEHOLDER: preview only. PENDING: unconfirmed. PROHIBITED: unsupported/fabricated as factual output. A claim may change class only with dated evidence and an owner decision.

No fabricated reviews, ratings, urgency, discounts, credentials, guarantees, job counts, staff portraits or before/after proof. Stock media cannot represent documented customer results. Validate body copy, metadata, JSON-LD, alt text, legal pages and AI answers together. Scanner pass is not content approval.

Preserve cleaned move-out/checklist wording, cautious NDIS enquiry wording, insurance verification notices and request-oriented booking wording. Residual stronger claims elsewhere are audit findings, not permission to reintroduce them.
