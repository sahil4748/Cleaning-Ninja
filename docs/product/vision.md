# Vision

Owner-approved direction, 21 September 2026. Cleaning Ninja is an established operating business receiving a flagship digital rebuild. The creative concept is **IMMACULATE TRANSITION**: visual noise → precision → calm.

The experience should be premium, cinematic, clean, warm, calm, precise, memorable and conversion-focused. Preserve/refine the olive identity. Express care through legibility, credible service information and an honest lead journey. These are product requirements, not evidence for unverified service claims.

Success measures proposed for approval: successful qualified enquiries, clear service/area discovery, accessible mobile completion and fast initial rendering. Current conversion baseline is unknown because no analytics implementation was found.
